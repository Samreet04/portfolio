The Roots

Hello, I'm Samreet Kaur. Born in the vibrant heart of Punjab. Growing up around art in everyday life taught me to see stories in details and meaning in simplicity.

The Craft

My journey began with painting, where I learned to see emotions in visuals. I am a UI/UX Designer who approaches digital canvases like physical ones.

The Personality

My greatest strength lies in my duality: the wild imagination of an artist and the logic of a designer. One side explores freely, the other shapes with intention.
